01 Telkom University

Learning to ask where it breaks

I studied Industrial Engineering in Bandung. The course trained one question into me: where can this process fail, and how do you make sure it doesn't fail the same way twice? Statistics, operations research and production systems were the tools. The habit stayed.

02 Lab and aerospace

Teaching, then building

For more than two years I taught CAD as a laboratory assistant and kept the lab running. Then I spent a summer at PT Dirgantara Indonesia on the NC212i aircraft program, tracking timelines and reading operational data to find what was slowing production down.

03 Market research

Into the markets

In July 2026 I started as a Junior Macro Analyst, writing research for professional traders. Every day meant futures positioning, economic releases and central bank decisions. In this work a wrong number costs trust, so every figure gets checked against its source and every correction becomes a written rule.

A wrong number costs more than a late one. So I check first, and I write down what I learn.
04 AI, done carefully

Building the system around the work

I set up the AI side of my research the way an engineer would: memory files, rules, checklists and a fact log, so the model works inside a clear structure instead of guessing. Now I'm adding SQL and dashboards through public-data projects, and looking for a data analyst role where careful work matters.
